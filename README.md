# VerifyX 

**Universal, Non-Transferable Identity for the Stellar Ecosystem**

VerifyX is a protocol-level identity infrastructure that allows users to verify once and use their identity across **any Stellar dApp** — securely, privately, and permanently.

Alongside the protocol, **VerifyX provides a powerful SDK** that developers can integrate into their applications to instantly verify users, read reputation, enforce compliance, and enable trusted interactions across the Stellar ecosystem.

---

## The Problem

Decentralized finance enables trustless transactions, yet real financial systems still require trusted identity. Today's identity solutions are fragmented and inefficient. Centralized providers lock user data in silos, create single points of failure, and expose users to privacy risks and data breaches. Per-dApp KYC forces users to repeat verification across multiple platforms, increasing costs, friction, and user drop-off while preventing reputation portability. Transferable identity NFTs also fail because they can be sold, stolen, or separated from their true owner, making them unreliable for compliance and trust. As a result, Web3 lacks a universal, secure, and portable identity layer.

---

## The Solution — VerifyX

VerifyX is a universal, non-transferable identity protocol built natively on the Stellar blockchain. It provides users with a soulbound, wallet-bound identity  that can be verified once and used across any Stellar application. VerifyX enables instant identity verification, on-chain reputation tracking, and privacy-preserving credential proofs using zero-knowledge technology. The protocol also includes a secure recovery mechanism that allows users to migrate their identity if their wallet is compromised, ensuring identity continuity without sacrificing ownership. Through the VerifyX SDK, developers can seamlessly integrate identity verification into their applications, making trust, compliance, and reputation portable across the entire Stellar ecosystem.
